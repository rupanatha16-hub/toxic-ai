What is TOXIC AI?

TOXIC AI is a free AI assistant that you can install on your phone like an app. Chat with AI, and use ready-made tools for writing, studying, coding, daily work, photos, PDFs, voice and video ideas, all in one place.

What you can do

AI chatAsk anything in simple language.
8 AI modesAssistant, Teacher, Coder, Writer, Friend, Research, Reasoning, Translator.
StudioDozens of tools for everyday work.
Browser toolsMany tools work on your phone, with no AI call.
Custom instructionsTell the AI how you like answers.
Light and DarkPick the theme you prefer.

Why "multiple AI brains"?

Behind the app, several AI providers work together. If one is busy or slow, another can answer, so you get a reply more reliably.
