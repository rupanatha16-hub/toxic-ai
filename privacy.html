1. Who we are

TOXIC AI ("TOXIC AI", "the app", "we", "us") is a free AI assistant that runs in your browser and can be installed on your phone as a web app. It is built and operated by Rupnath as an independent developer. The app is available at rupanatha16-hub.github.io/toxic-ai.

This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we use

InformationWhy we use it
Name, email address and profile photo (only if you sign in with Google)To show your account in the app and to let you sign in and out.
Messages, and any photo or file you attach to a chat or AI toolTo create the reply or result you asked for.
Voice, if you use the microphoneTo turn speech into text. This is done by your browser's speech service.
Settings you choose (theme, AI mode, custom instructions, favourites, notes, to-do items)To remember your preferences on your device.
Basic technical data such as IP address and request timeKept by our hosting provider in standard logs for security and reliability.

We do not ask for your phone number, address, payment details or contacts.

3. Google user data

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app receives only your basic profile: your name, email address and profile photo. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

This information is used only to sign you in and show your account inside the app. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with other companies. TOXIC AI's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can continue as a guest instead, and you can remove the app's access at any time (see "Your choices").

4. AI providers that process your messages

When you ask the AI something, your message is sent from the app to our server, which forwards it to an AI provider so that a reply can be created. Providers we use or may use:

These companies process the content under their own terms and privacy policies. Because AI replies are created by third parties, please do not enter passwords, bank or card details, government ID numbers or other highly sensitive information into the app. The name of the provider that answered is shown under each reply.

5. Storage and retention

Your chat history, settings, notes, to-do items and favourites are stored on your own device, in your browser's local storage. They stay there until you delete them, clear your browser data, or uninstall the app. At this time, the app does not keep a separate user database of your chats on our server.

Many Studio tools (such as the calculator, photo editor and image to PDF) work inside your browser, so the files you use with them are processed on your device.

Technical logs kept by our hosting provider are retained according to that provider's own policy.

6. Sharing, advertising and sale of data

We do not sell your personal information. We share the content of your requests only with the AI and hosting providers listed above, and only to deliver the service you asked for. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

The app does not currently show advertisements. If advertising or paid plans are added in future, we will update this policy before they begin.

7. Security

Connections between the app and our server use HTTPS. Secret keys for the AI providers are kept on the server and are never placed in the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

  • Delete your chats: Settings > Privacy > "Saari chats delete karo".
  • Sign out: Settings > Log out. You can also use the app as a guest.
  • Remove Google access: open your Google Account > Security > "Your connections to third-party apps and services", then remove TOXIC AI.
  • Clear everything on your device: clear the site data for the app in your browser settings.
  • Questions or requests about your data: email us at the address in the Contact section.

9. Children

TOXIC AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a significant change, we will make it clear inside the app or on this page.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or your information, write to toxicai.support@gmail.com.
